Selected work

A few cases from my current role. Each one started with a confusing symptom and ended with a measured answer and a decision.

Slow LLM responses: the vendor's fix targeted the wrong component

Situation
LLM responses were slow. The vendor had scaled the auth service from 1 to 10 replicas.
Evidence
An approved concurrency test (180 requests, 0 errors) and vLLM metrics showed the bottleneck was long-prompt prefill on a single GPU.
Decision
Management approved my proposal to serve a second model replica on GPU capacity I had measured as allocated but idle.

A recurring GPU-node outage traced to a silent NVMe hang

Situation
A GPU node kept going down, again and again.
Evidence
A minute-by-minute timeline from Prometheus, kernel and BMC logs traced the outage to an NVMe disk hanging silently in RAID1.
Decision
I ran the escalation in English with the vendor; they confirmed the finding and hot-swapped the disk.

Monitoring a vendor-managed AI platform without installing agents

Situation
A vendor-managed AI platform had to join central monitoring without installing agents on it.
Evidence
I built a read-only federation bridge over SSH (Python stdlib, systemd).
Result
The platform joined central monitoring, and ingested series dropped from ~25,000 to ~1,900.

A domain-wide DNS move, decided on evidence

Situation
A domain-wide DNS move to Cloudflare needed an approved plan.
Evidence
I documented the evidence and laid out three options with their trade-offs.
Decision
The recommended option was chosen and migrated with zero downtime.
