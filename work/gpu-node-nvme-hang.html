A recurring GPU-node outage traced to a silent NVMe hang

Production case study. Internal hostnames and identifiers are omitted.

Situation
A GPU node kept going down, again and again.
Evidence
A minute-by-minute timeline from Prometheus, kernel and BMC logs traced the outage to an NVMe disk hanging silently in RAID1.
Decision
I ran the escalation in English with the vendor; they confirmed the finding and hot-swapped the disk.

How I worked

  • The GPU node of a vendor-managed AI platform kept going NotReady: the container runtime froze and the platform's UI returned 503. A reboot brought it back, and then it happened again.
  • I built a minute-by-minute timeline from our own Prometheus data and lined it up with the kernel journal and the BMC (hardware management) logs. I wrote a small Python parser for the BMC log format.
  • One NVMe disk in a RAID1 array hung without returning errors. Multipath kept requeueing the I/O, so RAID never dropped the disk and every write blocked.
  • I sent the vendor an evidence pack in English with a proposed action. They confirmed the diagnosis and hot-swapped the disk.
  • Afterwards I added alerts for RAID state, a missing spare and stuck I/O, and wrote a postmortem and a runbook.

Takeaway

A reboot that makes the symptom go away is not a root cause. A good evidence pack saves time on both sides of a support ticket.
