A domain-wide DNS move, decided on evidence

Production case study. Internal hostnames and identifiers are omitted.

Situation
A domain-wide DNS move to Cloudflare needed an approved plan.
Evidence
I documented the evidence and laid out three options with their trade-offs.
Decision
The recommended option was chosen and migrated with zero downtime.

Why it is here

DNS changes touch everything and are hard to undo under pressure. Writing the options down with their trade-offs turned a risky change into a decision people could approve, and the move itself went through without downtime.
