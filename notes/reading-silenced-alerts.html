Reading silenced alerts as a separate list

18 Nov 2026

During a monitoring review, known issues were hidden from the active alert view because they were silenced. Reading only the active list hid existing context and made known issues look like new critical findings whenever they resurfaced.

I changed how I review alerts: I inspect active alerts and silenced alerts as two separate lists. In that review, I renewed four silences nearing expiry and intentionally dropped one that was no longer needed.

A silenced alert is not a resolved alert. Keeping it visible in its own list separates known risk from new incidents.
