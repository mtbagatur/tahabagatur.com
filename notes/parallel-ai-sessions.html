Avoiding duplicate work across parallel AI sessions

18 Nov 2026

Running several AI sessions in parallel speeds up execution, but without clear ownership they can solve the same problem twice. In one day, my parallel sessions duplicated work twice: two pull requests were opened for a single fix, and hours were logged twice against the same work item.

I adopted a two-line rule: I assign a single owner to each task before starting, and I cross-check all parallel session outputs at the end of the day. In the next 8.5 hours of logged work, duplicate effort dropped to 0.

Parallelism without ownership just multiplies cleanup. A clear owner at the start and a cross-check at the close keep fast work clean.
