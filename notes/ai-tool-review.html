How I screened six AI tools before adoption

18 Nov 2026

I evaluated six trending AI tools for my workflow. I adopted one open-source security-audit skill only after reading every file, pinning its commit, and copying it manually instead of running npx. From a second bundle of ~300 skills and ~40 hooks, I cherry-picked four parts by hand rather than installing the package wholesale. I rejected the remaining four tools.

Three questions decided each case: who maintains the code, which exact commit runs, and where do prompts and telemetry go? One free model gateway routed prompts to a third party for training; a local proxy shipped with telemetry enabled by default.

Adopting an AI tool is a supply-chain and data-flow decision before it is a productivity choice.
