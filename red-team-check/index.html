What I test

The check covers common ways a chat or RAG feature can expose data, follow hostile instructions or behave unsafely.

  • Prompt injection and instruction overrides

    Direct instructions, role-play, obfuscated text, and hostile instructions in pasted documents or support tickets.

  • Prompt and data leaks

    System prompt contents, other users’ data, conversation history, environment values and secret-like strings.

  • Unsafe links and commitments

    Phishing destinations, risky downloads, and replies that appear to approve refunds, waive fees or make binding promises.

  • Scope, cost and user safety

    Off-topic or oversized requests, political or abusive content, and medical emergencies that need escalation.

The built-in set contains 40 English-language attacks. This is a baseline, not a guarantee; multi-turn and tailored indirect-injection tests need a separate scope.

What you receive

  • A two-day black-box test of one AI feature on staging, with test data and your written permission.
  • A readable report with evidence and a practical fix for every finding.
  • The attack set as a JUnit regression test your CI can use as a release gate.

The check is read-only. Your team reviews and approves any change before it is made.
