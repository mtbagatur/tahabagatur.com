About

I’m Taha Bagatur, an AI infrastructure and DevOps engineer. In my day job I run LLM and computer-vision workloads in production for a large retail group: GPU inference servers, RAG services, an edge fleet of 50+ devices, monitoring and drift detection. I also own the DevOps platform underneath: CI/CD, Linux servers, Kubernetes, observability and backups.

Alongside that, I take a small number of fixed-price projects for remote teams.

How I work

  • Read-only first. Reviews never change your systems; fixes happen only after written approval.
  • Evidence, not opinions. Every finding says what was measured, so your team can check it.
  • Your data stays yours. Personal data is masked in anything I keep, and access is removed when we finish.
  • Agencies: I work behind the scenes; you keep the client relationship.
